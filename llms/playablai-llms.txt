## What is Playabl?

Playabl is an AI-powered game creation platform that enables users to build web games and interactive experiences through natural language conversation. Users describe their game idea in plain English, and Playabl's AI generates playable games instantly. No coding, game engine knowledge, or technical skills required.

## How Playabl Works

1. Describe your game idea in plain English
2. Playabl's AI generates a playable prototype
3. Refine your game by chatting with the AI
4. Publish and share your game on the web
